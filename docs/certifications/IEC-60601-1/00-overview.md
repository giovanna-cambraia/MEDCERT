# IEC 60601-1 — General Safety & Essential Performance

General standard for basic safety and essential performance of medical
electrical equipment. This folder holds the essential performance
definition, safety requirements derived from it, and test reports.

- essential_performance/ — definition of essential performance per device function
- safety_requirements/   — derived safety requirements (traces to ISO 14971 risk controls)
- test_reports/           — verification evidence against clause requirements

Particular (-2-x) and collateral (-1-x) standards applicable to the specific
device are TBD until the device type/intended use is defined.

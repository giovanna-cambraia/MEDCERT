# ISO 14971 — Risk Management

Medical analog of ARP4761. Drives the IEC 62304 software safety
classification and the IEC 60601-1 safety requirements.

- risk_management_plan/  — RMP (scope, criteria, roles)
- hazard_analysis/        — hazard identification, hazardous situations, harms
- fmea/                    — FMEA worksheets (component/function level)
- risk_management_file/   — the aggregated RMF (risk summary, residual risk
                             evaluation, RM report) built from the above
